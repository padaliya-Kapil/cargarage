Your data

Privacy

Plain-English summary. Have this reviewed for your local privacy law before launch.

What we collect

When you book or send a message we store your name, phone number, email (if given), vehicle details and anything you write in the notes. We also keep a record of the work done on your car and invoices.

Why

To confirm and manage your booking, contact you about your vehicle, keep a service history, and meet our tax and warranty obligations. We do not sell your data or use it for third-party advertising.

How long

Service records are kept for as long as the law requires for business records. You can ask us to correct your details or delete them where the law allows.

Cookies

The public website sets no tracking cookies. The staff area uses one essential sign-in cookie.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact the garage by phone or email listed on the contact section.
