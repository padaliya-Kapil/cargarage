Good questions

FAQ

Will servicing here void my new-car warranty?

No. We follow the manufacturer's schedule, use parts that meet spec and stamp your logbook, so your warranty stays intact.

How long will my car take?

Most services, brakes, tyres and batteries are done the same day. Bigger jobs like clutches get a time estimate with your quote.

Do I have to pay before you start?

No. We call you with an itemised quote first, and you pay when you pick the car up.

Can I wait while you work?

For short jobs like oil changes and batteries, yes — grab a chai. For longer jobs we'll message you when it's ready.

Which payment methods do you take?

Card, cash and bank transfer.
